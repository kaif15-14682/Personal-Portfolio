ProstadeNet: prostate cancer grading

My bachelor's thesis. A CNN that sorts tissue images into the six ISUP cancer grades, built to help pathologists grade faster and more consistently.

ProstadeNet

From slide to grade

One tissue tile goes through the cleaning pipeline, the dataset grows with augmentation, and the tuned network lands its accuracy. The tile is drawn in the browser as a stand-in for a real slide.

512 × 512
  1. Raw tile512 × 512, speckle noise, flat contrast
  2. ResizeDown to 224 × 224 for the network
  3. Median filterRemoves speckle noise
  4. CLAHEBoosts local contrast
  5. AugmentationColour, contrast, brightness, sharpness
  6. Train and tune10-step ablation study
Images53,080
Test accuracy99.83%
