Machine learning2024 – 2025

ProstadeNet: prostate cancer grading

My bachelor's thesis. A CNN that sorts tissue images into the six ISUP cancer grades, built to help pathologists grade faster and more consistently.

  • Cleaned 10,616 images and grew the set to 53,080 with augmentation.
  • Tuned the network through a 10-step ablation study, from 96.07% to 99.83% test accuracy.
  • Beat five pretrained models and checked it with 5-fold cross-validation (97.28% to 98.37%).
  • Python
  • TensorFlow
  • Keras
  • OpenCV
  • Google Colab

Supervised by Ms. Nazmun Nessa Moon. Full report available on request.
