Thesis · ProstadeNet

From slide to grade.

One tissue tile passes through the network. Each layer's feature maps light up, then the six grade scores settle on a prediction.

Softmaxsix ISUP grades

Predicted: grade 3 · 97%Illustrative scores, not real model output

  1. Tile inA 224 × 224 tissue image
  2. Conv 32 + poolSmall filters respond to edges and nuclei
  3. Conv 64 + poolEach block halves the maps and looks wider
  4. Conv 128 + poolDeeper filters respond to larger structures
